Project Brief

Project Brief

This directional research (DR) focuses on the importance of vernacular languages in digital products. The aim of this research is to study company practices around making products vernacular and the research practices surrounding it and to understand the importance of language and socio-cultural context during user research for the next billion users. This research gives a direction and perspective to how and why vernacularisation of the product should be approached.

Top Findings

Top Findings

Approaches to vernacularising a platform:

Translation vs Transliteration:
Users have different proficiencies in reading the script of a language vs. understanding the language itself. In some case, regional language maybe written in an english script “isko share kare” or english words may need to written in the regional script “शेयर”

Colloquial language:
During translation, it is important to consider the colloquial language of a region as opposed to a strict translation which may not be understood by the people because of its “pureness”. Eg, Share in Hindi is "Shaja", a word people don't use in daily language and hence is not understood by the masses

Context of use:
Interaction/ communication vs reading/writing: Users have different preferences for language when interacting with other users on the platform vs.
the the content which is a part of the interface.

Benefits of vernacular language in digital products

Acquisition:
To acquire users by marketing it as a vernacular product

Adoption:
To increase user adoption of the platform by making it inclusive especially for audience with low digital literacy

Retention:
To increase the retention on the platform by leveraging vernacular language as an extra/ added feature for convenience. Targeted at digitally literate users who can complete tasks in English but prefer their regional language

Impact

Impact

• This study provided clarity on benchmarking vernacular approach

• Based on these insights, a hybrid approach of translation and transliteration of the core user journey was executed

• The research outputs also contributed to product prioritisation and decision-making on vernacular execution for the following quarter's planning
