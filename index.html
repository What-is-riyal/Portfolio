I’m an HCI researcher with 4+ years of experience working with people with low tech literacy

I am currently pursuing my masters in Human Computer Interaction at Carnegie Mellon University. I work with Professor Tom Costello on understanding AI persuasion and belief systems. My work sits at the intersection of human - AI communication, digital trust, and cross cultural research.
Before CMU, I spent over four years leading research projects at Razorpay, Microsoft, and Meesho - helping low tech literate and everyday users make sense of complex systems. At CMU, I’m extending this foundation through coursework in Augmenting Intelligence, Responsible AI, and Ethics & Policy in Computing, exploring how responsible design and research can guide the next generation of intelligent systems.
