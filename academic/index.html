About

I study how people understand, trust, and make decisions with AI systems.

I am a research assistant at Carnegie Mellon University, working with Aarti Singh and Samsara Foubert at the NSF AI Institute for Societal Decision Making. I received a Master of Human-Computer Interaction from CMU in 2026. From 2021 to 2025 I worked as a user researcher and product designer at Razorpay and Microsoft, and I hold a Bachelor of Design from IIT Guwahati.

My interests fall into three broad areas:

  1. Reliance. How people decide when, and how far, to rely on AI.
  2. Influence. How AI systems shape what people believe and choose, through persuasion, deception, or the information they rank and show.
  3. Governance. How institutions can measure these effects and hold the systems that produce them accountable.

I use controlled experiments, qualitative analysis, and simulation, and I draw on my industry training in interviews, surveys, and usability research.
