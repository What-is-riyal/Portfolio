About me

Researcher by practice. Compulsively curious by nature.

I started as a product designer and moved into research when I realized the quality of a design is capped by the quality of the evidence underneath it. Since then, I’ve spent 4+ years at Microsoft, Razorpay, and Meesho asking questions on behalf of people products too often overlook.

My work moves between foundational studies, evaluative research, and product strategy. The common thread is making complicated systems feel more legible—to the people using them and to the teams deciding what to build next.

Right now, I’m completing my MHCI at Carnegie Mellon and researching how people make sense of AI, persuasion, and credibility. Trust matters in that work, but so do usefulness, access, language, and the everyday realities around a product.
