I am a research assistant at Carnegie Mellon University. I work with Aarti Singh and Samsara Foubert at the NSF AI Institute for Societal Decision Making. I received a Master of Human-Computer Interaction from CMU in 2026. From 2021 to 2025 I worked as a user researcher and product designer at Razorpay and Microsoft, and I hold a Bachelor of Design from IIT Guwahati.

I study how people understand, trust, and make decisions with AI systems. My current work covers three questions:

  • When should a person rely on AI for a given task, and how can that decision be modeled?
  • How do language models persuade and deceive, and how does disclosing those tactics change what users believe and do?
  • How can regulators and researchers measure the effects of recommender systems and synthetic media, and constrain them without restricting lawful speech?

I use controlled experiments, qualitative analysis, and simulation, and I draw on my industry training in interviews, surveys, and usability research.
