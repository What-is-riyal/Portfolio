About me

I make sense of messy human problems.

I started as a product designer, then moved into research because I wanted to understand the problem before designing a solution. Over the past four years, I’ve worked at Microsoft, Razorpay, and Meesho.

I plan and run interviews, surveys, usability studies, and workshops. I’ve worked with low-tech-literacy users, people across cultures and languages, and teams building trust in AI.

I’m currently completing a master’s in Human-Computer Interaction at Carnegie Mellon. My recent academic work looks at how AI changes people’s opinions, how errors in automatic captions affect speakers, and how people decide when to use AI.

Off the clock
