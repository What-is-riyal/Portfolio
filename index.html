I'm a user researcher. I help teams make decisions they can defend.

Over the past five years I've worked on research at Microsoft, Razorpay, and Meesho: strategic studies that shaped investment decisions, evaluative work that shipped, and foundational research on users the industry tends to overlook. I'm currently finishing the Master of Human-Computer Interaction at Carnegie Mellon, where I also assist on research into misinformation and AI persuasion.

The case studies below focus on how each study was designed and why, the decisions made along the way, and what changed because of the work. Where findings are under NDA, I say so and show the method.

Selected work

Microsoft · 2024–25 · Strategic synthesis

A point of view for Copilot in Outlook

A year of feature-by-feature Copilot research existed; a shared position didn't. I synthesized it into a white paper on what users need before they let an AI assistant act for them, with design guidelines and a pre-ship checklist teams could apply directly.

Microsoft · 2024 · Mixed-methods study

Why people were leaving Outlook on the mobile web

A team retention study built on 36 interviews across six user segments and a 300+ response validation survey. The analysis reframed the product's role inside the larger system of email accounts each person runs, and fed directly into the next semester's plan.

Razorpay · 2024 · Strategic research

Testing a consumer product bet before the company made it

Leadership was considering a consumer-facing rewards marketplace. I designed and ran a two-sided validation study, 25+ merchant interviews, 3 focus groups, and a 1000+ respondent survey, that reshaped the value proposition and informed the investment decision.

Razorpay · 2023 · Evaluative research

Evaluating a homepage redesign before a million people saw it

No research was planned for the redesign of a page with 1M+ daily visitors, so I proposed it. Six instruments in six weeks isolated what the new page communicated, layer by layer. 90% of the recommendations were adopted, and conversion rose 10% after launch.

Razorpay · 2021–22 · Design and research

Redesigning merchant onboarding around an existing mental model

Small merchants were abandoning a 14-minute compliance form. Working from 150+ support tickets, 50+ session recordings, and a role-play exercise, I rebuilt the flow as one plain question at a time. Conversion rose 51%, completion time fell to 5 minutes, and support tickets dropped 43%.

Meesho · 2020 · Foundational research

Language and bias in research for India's next billion users

As a research intern, I led a ten-week study on how language shapes both products and the research behind them, through 10 expert interviews. The report argued for a colloquial hybrid over pure translation, and one finding about practitioner bias became its own workshop.
