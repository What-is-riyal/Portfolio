MadAhead: a pass-the-story game

Co-founder · 2019–20 · shipped to the Google Play Store

MadAhead was a "pass the story" game: strangers write a story together, one chunk at a time, each person building on whatever the last person left them. I co-founded it, developed the strategy and the experience, and led a team of five designers and UX writers through to launch on the Play Store, collaborating with engineering on the way.

It taught me more about scope, shipping, and writing for an audience that talks back than any course did, and it remains the only project of mine with reviews.
