Working papers

CORDA: a common testbed for alternative feed rankers

With the CORDA Democracy research team

Working paper · Exploratory results

Design and evidence

Six ranking rules — chronological, engagement, bridging, homophily, outrage downranking, and toxicity downranking — run against an identical 3,000-agent population and COVID-19 seed corpus. Shared measures: engagement, conversation size, cross-group approval, moral outrage, toxicity, and conspiracy content.

Current results come from a single 1,123-tick run. In that run the chronological condition sustained the highest cross-group approval score, ahead of the intended bridging ranker. Repeated runs are needed before the comparison supports conclusions about ranker performance.

Code and supplementary material · Simulation dashboard

Deceptive tactics in competitive LLM agents

Leander Maben, Danqing Wang, Priyal Shrivastava

Working paper · Exploratory study

Design and contribution

Werewolf used as a controlled setting for studying how language-model agents persuade, conceal information, and coordinate when reward depends on winning. A Qwen3-4B agent trained for 200 RL steps; checkpoints 0, 19, 39, and 59 evaluated over three games each, against five baseline models.

My primary contribution is a qualitative taxonomy of nine deceptive tactics drawn from the game transcripts, extending the MentalManip taxonomy with friendly betrayal.
