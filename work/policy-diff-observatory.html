01 · Research problem

Missing policy history

AI companies publish rules that describe acceptable use, model behavior, and safety commitments. These pages are often updated in place. A reader can see the current version but may have difficulty finding what changed, when it changed, or how the earlier language differed.

Research need

Researchers and policymakers need a durable record to study how company commitments evolve. That record must preserve the original text, show the exact edit, and make every comparison independently checkable.

Research question

How can changes to public AI governance documents be recorded while reserving interpretation for researchers?

02 · Method

Version-tracking pipeline

The prototype retrieves a fixed list of public documents and compares each successful retrieval with the last trusted copy. The intended operating schedule is one check each week.

  1. 01

    Retrieve

    Fetch the public document from the company’s official page.

  2. 02

    Check

    Reject pages that are empty, unusually short, or much smaller than the stored version.

  3. 03

    Compare

    Align the old and new text and record additions and removals.

  4. 04

    Preserve

    Save the new snapshot, the exact difference, source links, and repository history.

Evidence rule

A failed or doubtful retrieval creates a visible source-health warning and retains the last trusted version. Only a validated retrieval enters the research record.

03 · Research record

Evidence attached to each change

  • Changed text: the exact additions and removals
  • Live source: the company’s current public document
  • Archived source: an earlier Wayback Machine capture when a usable copy exists
  • Comparison date: the version and date used as the baseline
  • Repository history: the commit that preserves the update
  • Source health: the latest successful or failed retrieval status

04 · Research interface

Policy Diff Observatory

The interface filters the record by company, document, date, and document type. Each entry preserves the changed text, comparison date, source link, and collection status.

Prototype status

The source-tracking pipeline and interface are implemented locally. Public launch depends on committing the scheduled monitor and verifying successful recurring runs.

05 · Interpretation

Coverage and limits

  • The Observatory covers public documents that can be retrieved as reliable text from a stable URL.
  • Browser-only, blocked, or incomplete pages enter a review queue for manual collection.
  • Each entry presents the textual change and its supporting evidence for researcher interpretation.
  • Historical coverage depends on the availability and quality of archived pages.
  • Researchers interpret each change in its legal, technical, and institutional context.
