Outlook Mobile Web Retention Study

A UXR study to identify reasons for

Background

  • While the transition of Outlook users using mail apps is a natural one, as users get more comfortable with using smart phones over the years and want better capabilities. The decline in the usage of mobile browser (Outlook.com) saw a decline within the consumer users that was more than expected.

  • We wanted to better understand what is causing this decline within the mobile web consumer users (non-commercial usage) of Outlook.

Reflecting on existing research, data, telemetry, UX audits & recognising knowledge gaps to structure & prioritise research efforts

Hypothesis Workshop

  1. Multiple team members across leadership, PM's and engineering had various questions & hypothesis about the reason they believed was causing decline in the consumer users.

  2. Secondary research was conducted across data science reports, UX audits, marketing reports and other UX research reports that were conducted to better understand what we already knew.

  3. These findings were presented to the entire team in a hypothesis workshop, while collaborating with the data scientists, PM's & designers involved.

  4. Furthermore, the same information was used to identify knowledge gaps and narrow down on research investments to be made for this enquiry to learn about the missing pieces.

Research Objectives (based on workshop outputs)

  • To identify the factors that affect the retention of various types of Outlook mobile browser consumer users, and what are the main drivers and barriers for user loyalty and churn. (6 user segments were identified through the hypothesis workshop basis usage behaviour across various Outlook end-points).

  • We also want to learn what is the value proposition of the Outlook mobile browser seen by various user groups. Understand the role Outlook mobile browser plays as compared to other end-points when accessing Outlook.

  • Identify opportunities for improvements that can help with user engagement & retention.

  • Get early feedback on investments we are making: multi-account, Shortcut from screen icon (PWA) & push notifications.

  • The goal of this research is to inform the product strategy and roadmap for future product decisions & feature developments.

Method: Moderated 1:1 user interviews, followed by a quantitative survey to further validate the findings with each co-hort group

1:1 user interviews were conducted on Usertesting.com

36 participants : 4 to 6 users per user group identified through usage patterns via data.

We created a Survey Map to design a survey for Zoho

Take Aways:

There are 4 types of email accounts: primary (main account), secondary (alternate account), tertiary (junk filled), abandoned (dormant).

Users prefer to access these varied types of accounts on specific end points chosen basis the content & purpose of each account.

Mini plays an important role in the Outlook eco-system acting as an end-point for providing access to Hotmail account users (that have become secondary & tertiary in nature over a period), a buffer for OM & 3P users from churning when apps are down (slow syncing & loading emails + attachments on OM & 3P. By providing a reliable & familiar experience to OM while being storage saving for less tech savvy & storage constrained users with heavy & lagging email apps, also providing familiarity for users that are comfortable with OWA experiences, etc.

Key Take Aways

😭Some Key Challenges faced by users of Mini OWA are:
having cluttered inboxes that are hard to organize and manage, bombarded with non-contextual + unwanted notifications & ads in the past year leading to migration out of Mini OWA, accounts compromised with multiple hacker attempts and spams, cannot access offline use, lack of purpose for tertiary accounts, experience gaps and need for better Work Life balance that is provided by google while Outlook has become the Work email provider in organizations, changing devices leads users to explore newer email apps, etc.

👍🏼Some Key Opportunity areas include: helping users in clearing out junk from their inboxes and giving these accounts a refreshed purpose, emphasizing on the benefits of creating an Outlook account for access to a professional M365 eco-system, prioritization of important communications & customized notifications of emails from certain senders, DND focused mode for certain users, filling in the market gap for a good multi-account experience agnostic of the service provider, better hand-offs between OM, 3P & Mini, minimal & contextually relevant notifications & ads, shortcuts via: browser favorites, PWA & always staying signed in, etc.
