05 Razorpay · 2021–22

Why did merchants give up before their first payment?

Redesigning KYC for low-tech-literacy merchants, lifting conversion by 51%

Ticket analysis, 150+ Empathy workshop Usability

The turn These merchants had all opened bank accounts before, with the same documents. A role play of that counter conversation became the design: one question at a time, with the reason for asking.

06 Meesho · 2020

What gets lost when research is in English?

Foundational research on language and bias in designing for India's next billion users

10 expert interviews Foundational research

The turn Textbook translation made the app harder, and pure transliteration read as noise. The experts converged on the colloquial mix people actually speak, so the report argued for a hybrid.

About me

I've spent 4+ years leading research at Razorpay, Microsoft, and Meesho: strategic studies that shaped roadmaps, evaluative work that shipped, and end-to-end redesigns. Much of it has been helping everyday users and people with low tech literacy make sense of complex systems, and helping teams act on what they said.

At Carnegie Mellon (MHCI, Class of 2026) I'm extending that foundation toward AI: coursework in Augmenting Intelligence, Responsible AI, and Ethics & Policy in Computing, exploring how responsible design and research can guide the next generation of intelligent systems.
