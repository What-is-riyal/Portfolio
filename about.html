About

I ask "why" one more time than is polite.

I'm Priyal, a user researcher. Across five years at Microsoft, Razorpay, and Meesho, I've asked that question on behalf of people who rarely get asked anything at all: small merchants stuck on compliance forms, first-time internet users shopping in their second language, and everyday people deciding whether to trust an AI assistant with their inbox.

I started as a product designer and moved into research when I noticed that the quality of my designs was capped by the quality of the evidence underneath them. Since then the work has ranged from foundational studies that reframed company strategy to evaluative work that shipped, and most recently a white paper on what people need before they let an AI act on their behalf.

Right now I'm at Carnegie Mellon, finishing the Master of Human-Computer Interaction (class of 2026). Alongside the degree, I work as a research assistant in the Viewpoints Lab with Prof. Tom Costello, on misinformation and AI persuasion. The thread I want to keep pulling for the next decade: how people decide to trust automated systems, and who gets left out when those systems are built without asking.
