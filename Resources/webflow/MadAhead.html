A collaborative story creation experience
A digital version of the "pass the story" game aimed to create a new kind of social activity to improve imagination, spontaneous thinking and social bonding

The Brief

Create an online experience for "pass the story" game and devise a strategy by converting the idea into a product which can be positioned appropriately and provides value.

Mission
statement

“To nurture creative and imaginative thinking capacity in every individual through social collaboration."

Background

The name MadAhead denotes the excitement of the journey they are about to embark on.

The product has a friendly but professional personality. The community of users are referred to as "madsters".

The core value of the product is the experience of writing a story together with each turn being an unexpected twist.
What does it do?
Builds imagination and spontaneous thinking with the help of collaborative story writing
Who is it for?
Primarily engages 15 to 30 year olds, though, can be enjoyed by everyone who enjoys mini adventures in their head :)
Why do we need it?
Forms of remote social interaction have become redundant and friends are running out of activities to do together. MadAhead can be played with friends & the community to get new perspectives and challenge your imagination!
How does it work?
It offers collaborative story writing with your friends and people from the community where each player writes one line to build the plot within a time limit
Visit madahead.com

Market Positioning

• I began by doing a market study to understand which products are providing similar benefits.
• Then, we decided its positioning to further develop the features and distinguish it from products with similar benefits.
The product is positioned as an intersection of collaborative entertainment and imagination building tool.
AI Dungeon
AI Dungeon is a text adventure game which uses artificial intelligence to generate content. Stories are based on adventure and along the lines of D&D.
writealong.io/
wikistory
writealong.io and wikistory are websites that let people add on infinite story threads but are targeted towards serious writers for idea generation.
HaikuJAM
HaikuJAM lets users comment on haiku threads to complete them. There is no time restriction and the threads are open to all.

Design elements

Final product

MadAhead, the name, indicates that the metaphorical journey the users are about to embark upon, is going to be "mad ahead".
The community members of MadAhead are called "madsters". They earn titles based on the reactions they get on their plot lines which gives insights about their own writing inclination.
Make rooms
Players have full control over the group they make to play the game. They can pick upto 5 players with a combination of their friends and other Madsters
Game play
Once a minimum of one player has joined the room, the creator can start the game whenever they want. During the game, players can react to each other's plot line. Based on the reactions recieved, top performing players get titles "Jokester", "Twister", or "Sinister"
Publish and read stories
When the game ends, players can choose to publish their story with a title and keywords relevant to the plot. These stories get posted on the community wall where any madster can read it. They can search stories by titles, keywords and players.
Leaderboard
The leaderboard shows the player's stats - XP, Badges & Titles and their standing among the entire community and among their friends. They can see their overall ranking and ranking by title.

Future of the product & my learnings

~ This product is currently under development and undergoing cycles of iteration. More functionalities are planned for later versions which are signed under an NDA! ~

This project was a great learning experience for me specially in terms of designing & arranging the files for developer handoffs. I made a few mistakes initially like not covering all the edge cases or finding new screens which need to be made to explain what I had in mind to the developers. At the end of it though, I have grown immensely in my product thinking and interface design skills
This product failed. We failed at GTM, bad. But oh what a learning experience! We had a few 100 downloads and pivoted multiple times (from gaming to educational tool) before we sunsetted the product. It was a fun ride and I learned no entertainment product will mean anything till people don't talk about it. Lots more to say about this, happy to discuss it over coffee!
