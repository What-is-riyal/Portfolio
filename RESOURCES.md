## What each resource is and how to use it

### webflow/ — current portfolio pages
These are Webflow HTML exports of Priyal's live portfolio. Each `.html` file is one page; its `_files/` folder contains the associated assets.

**How to use:** For each case study or the homepage, start by reading the corresponding HTML. Run the inventory step from CLAUDE.md — extract every claim, number, quote, visual reference, and attribution. This inventory is your source of truth. Do not add facts that aren't here or that Priyal explicitly supplies in the session.

Pages and what they correspond to:
- `homepage.html` — current homepage
- `Play.html` — the Play page of the portfolio (not a case study)
- `D2C.html` — Razorpay D2C case study
- `Razorpay Website Evaluation.html` — Razorpay website evaluation case study
- `Razorpay Onboarding Redesign.html` — Razorpay onboarding redesign case study
- `Vernacular languages in digital products.html` — vernacular languages case study

### All projects - Mobile Web Retention Study for Outlook consumers.html — Khyati's case study
This is a fully written case study for the same project Priyal worked on, written from Priyal's collaborator Khyati's point of view.

**How to use:** Read it for two things — (1) **project depth**: what the study was, what was found, what changed, what the methods were, what the timeline looked like. This gives you a full picture of the project's substance. (2) **structural reference**: how a case study at this level of detail is organized.

**Critical constraint:** This is Khyati's version. When you write Priyal's version, everything must be from Priyal's point of view — her role, her contributions, her analytical moves, her perspective on what mattered. Where roles overlap, ask Priyal to clarify exactly what she owned vs. contributed to. Do not carry over Khyati's first-person framing, Khyati's analytical voice, or any claim about what Khyati specifically did. The project is shared; the case study is not.

### Transforming Outlook Copilots Value Proposition.pdf — Copilot white paper (NDA)
This is Priyal's final white paper on transforming Outlook Copilot's value proposition, produced during her Microsoft contract. It contains proprietary information and is under NDA.

**How to use:** Read it to understand Priyal's analytical approach, research lens, how she structured the argument, and the depth of thinking involved. Rough scale of the research: approximately 14–15 documents reviewed in total (confirm exact number with Priyal).

**Hard NDA constraint:** No specific company details, product names beyond what Priyal explicitly clears, internal data, or proprietary findings may appear in the portfolio case study. What *can* be written about: Priyal's methodology and research approach, her analytical framework, the types of questions she was investigating, how she synthesized across sources, the nature of the deliverable (a white paper). When in doubt about whether something is NDA-sensitive, ask Priyal — never make the judgment call yourself.

### white paper stickies.csv — working notes from the white paper research
A CSV export of the sticky notes Priyal used while working through the white paper research. Contains her in-progress thinking, themes she was tracking, and patterns across documents.

**How to use:** Read to understand the texture and volume of the research process — what she was noticing, how she was grouping ideas, how the thinking evolved. This is evidence of analytical depth that can inform how the case study describes her approach. It is working material, not a deliverable — do not quote it directly in the portfolio; use it to understand what to ask Priyal about and to write accurately about the process.

### white paper board.png — visual working board
A screenshot of the visual workspace Priyal used while developing the white paper.

**How to use:** Read alongside the stickies CSV to get a spatial sense of how the analysis was organized. Again, working material — informs your questions and your description of the process, does not get reproduced directly.

## Session start checklist

Before touching any file:

1. Read CLAUDE.md in full if you haven't in this session.
2. Read this document in full.
3. Confirm with Priyal which case study or page you're working on today.
4. Read the corresponding webflow HTML and run the inventory (CLAUDE.md step 1).
5. For the Outlook Mobile Retention study: read Khyati's HTML before starting.
6. For the white paper: read the PDF, stickies CSV, and board PNG before starting — then confirm NDA boundaries with Priyal before drafting anything.
7. List your gaps and open questions before proposing any structure.
