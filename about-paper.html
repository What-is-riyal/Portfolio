The question

How does evidence travel?

I keep returning to one question: how does careful research become a real decision?

02

Industry → policy

I started in industry, where I saw good evidence lose to timing, incentives, or the way a decision had already been framed. Research mattered most when it was connected to the decision from the beginning.

Evidence has to reach the decision while the decision can still change.

03

Why policy

That experience pulled me toward policy. Product decisions shape one system; policy determines the rules across many systems. It also makes the path from evidence to action more visible and more contestable.

04

Why academia

Academic research gives me time to define a question carefully, test an explanation, and make the evidence open to criticism. I also want to follow the work beyond publication.

Careful enough to trust. Useful enough to change a decision.

05

The space between

I want to work between research and implementation: close enough to the methods to defend the evidence, and close enough to institutions to understand what it would take for that evidence to change practice.
